Forgot Password Help

• Know your Username & Email: Click Forgot Password? on the Login tab to set a new password (at least 8 characters) immediately.
• Forgot your Email: Check any email address you usually use for badminton/apps, or ask a teammate to look up your exact @username in the RacketBuddy Feed.
• Using a new device/browser: Accounts created in local browser storage stay on that device unless saved/synced. If your account is not found or was inactive for 60 days, register a new account with an unused username and email.

Install RacketBuddy on Your Phone

Browsers block silent auto-install unless triggered manually from the browser menu:
• iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap Share (square with up arrow) → tap Add to Home Screen → tap Add.
• Android (Chrome): Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
